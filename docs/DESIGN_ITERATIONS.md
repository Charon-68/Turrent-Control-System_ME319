## Controller (feed-forward, PI loop, reference generator, acquisition)

### D8 Feedback gains and generator pole  (outcome: kept)

- **Specification.** An LTI compensator for G_p with analytic stability support (handout Sec. 4); no re-tuning per scenario.
- **Choice.** K_p = 4000 V/rad, T_i = 1 s, tau_f = 0.03 s and p = 20 rad/s 
- **Check.** Re-derived what they imply: crossover 4.10 rad/s, PM 68.5 deg, GM 16.1 dB, M_s 1.21, Routh limit for proportional-only 10 484 V/rad, full loop stable to 6.35x the design gain, type 2 with K_a = 3.99 1/s^2; the FVT parabola error confirmed by partial fractions (0.0150 rad both ways).
- **Revision / outcome.** No revision of the numbers.  The final-design ablations of the generator pole (below) show both neighbours slower in first hit, which is a check on p = 20, not a re-optimisation.
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) p_dev_10: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first +0.500 s [+0.468, +0.538] (p_Holm 0.003), d RMS +0.220 m [+0.185, +0.261] (p_Holm 0.003).
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) p_dev_30: 60/60 intercepted, 1 pulse miss(es), d t_first +0.192 s [+0.132, +0.255] (p_Holm 0.003), d RMS -0.031 m [-0.065, +0.013] (p_Holm 0.817).

### D5 Acquisition anti-windup (freeze_acq)  (outcome: kept, benefit withdrawn)

- **Specification.** In scenario A the clamp-limited slew overshot by ~3 m and P_hit crossed 0.99 only at t ~ 1.3 s, because the PI integrator had accumulated lag while the reference was not following the linear law.
- **Choice.** Pause the PI integrator while the cascade clamps are active (freeze_acq).
- **Check.** On exploration seeds MC201-240 mean first hit 1.621 -> 1.545 s (40/40, 0 misses).
- **Revision / outcome.** REVISED: the gain was confounded by the slip guard (D12).  In the final design the ablation no_freeze is -0.005 s [-0.022, +0.012], Holm p = 1.0: no effect.  The flag stays on as the conservative choice; the benefit is not claimed.
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) no_freeze: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first -0.005 s [-0.022, +0.012] (p_Holm 1.000), d RMS -0.002 m [-0.004, -0.000] (p_Holm 0.256).

### D6 Acceleration clamp from a current budget  (outcome: rejected (larger clamp))

- **Specification.** The handout states no current limit; acquisition needs an acceleration bound that a motor could deliver.
- **Choice.** acq_accel = 0.8 N K_t i_lim / J_eq with an ASSUMED i_lim = 10 A, i.e. 2.5 rad/s^2.
- **Check.** Relaxing to 5 rad/s^2 (about 16 A) cut mean first hit by 0.21 s with no misses on MC201-240.  Peak current is reported: 8.8 A on D6 at 100 Hz, up to 10.7-11.4 A in some robustness rows and 20 A with Kt -20 % / Ke +20 %.
- **Revision / outcome.** REJECTED as default: 5 rad/s^2 exceeds any current the handout can justify; the 20 % margin covers the reference acceleration only, so the budget is an acquisition guideline, not a hard current limit.  K.i_lim is the single place to change.

### D7 Braking-curve slew (acq_decel)  (outcome: rejected)

- **Specification.** Shave the exponential tail of the acquisition slew to hit earlier.
- **Choice.** Constant-deceleration braking beyond the linear zone (acq_decel).
- **Check.** Exploration seeds MC201-260: decel 1.5 gave -0.127 s mean first hit but 2 pulse misses; decel 3.0 gave +0.072 s.  Because that was a selection on the same seeds it was confirmed once on fresh seeds MC2000-2099.
- **Revision / outcome.** REJECTED: on the fresh seeds decel 1.5 was slower, +0.093 s [+0.080, +0.104], 0 vs 1 pulse misses; the exploration gain did not replicate.  Stays off (acq_decel = 0).
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) brake_slew: 60/60 intercepted, 2 pulse miss(es), d t_first +0.109 s [+0.086, +0.144] (p_Holm 0.003), d RMS -0.000 m [-0.004, +0.003] (p_Holm 1.000).

### D11 Mid-hold reference lead (lead_hold)  (outcome: rejected)

- **Specification.** The command is held for one call period; compensate by evaluating the reference half a period ahead ("ZOH compensation", my own idea).
- **Choice.** lead_hold: shift the reference by up to lead_max.
- **Check.** Reasoning: the beam is judged at the sampling instants and the exact inverse already tracks the nowcast, so the lead moves the aim point ahead of the target by (LOS rate x lead), about 2 m at 20 Hz.  Paired MC201-260 at 20 Hz: pointing RMS 0.996 m without vs 1.677 m with (+0.717 m [+0.572, +0.863]); at 100 Hz +0.07 m; on the HB family -0.053 m without it.
- **Revision / outcome.** REJECTED and removed: default lead_hold = false (the option stays for the ablation table).
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) lead_on: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first -0.001 s [-0.018, +0.016] (p_Holm 1.000), d RMS +0.072 m [+0.056, +0.089] (p_Holm 0.003).

### D16 Adaptive velocity feed-forward (adapt_ff)  (outcome: rejected)

- **Specification.** Robustness to plant mismatch: the velocity feed-forward term is ~ N K_e and is the sensitive one.
- **Choice.** adapt_ff: move integrator content into the velocity feed-forward gain.
- **Check.** No change on the nominal plant; in the worst mismatch corner (Kt -20 %, Ke +20 %) RMS stays at 3.7 m with a 20 A peak; in the opposite corner it moves 1.57 -> 1.50 m.
- **Revision / outcome.** REJECTED as default: stays off.  A better remedy (estimating K_e from the (V, omega) residual with persistent excitation) is future work, not claimed.
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) adapt_ff: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first +0.000 s [+0.000, +0.000] (p_Holm 1.000), d RMS -0.001 m [-0.001, +0.000] (p_Holm 0.662).

### ablation only Reference aim point: present bearing vs bearing t_p ahead  (outcome: adopted (alternative measured))

- **Specification.** The handout asks for a prediction horizon t_p = 0.5 s in the architecture.
- **Choice.** Cancel the t_p advance inside the reference generator so the beam follows the nowcast (K.aim_ahead = 0).
- **Check.** Ablation aim_tp (aim the beam t_p ahead, same fire rule) in outputs/ablations.txt.
- **Revision / outcome.** The literal reading of the prediction block is the alternative; the cancelled-advance design is kept (see the table row).
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) aim_tp: 19/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first +5.936 s [+4.846, +6.948] (p_Holm 0.003), d RMS +29.792 m [+26.359, +33.129] (p_Holm 0.003).

## Estimator and fire control

### D13 Adaptive process noise  (outcome: kept)

- **Specification.** Maneuvers inside blackouts and bang-bang jerk (the HB family) violate a fixed process-noise assumption.
- **Choice.** Scale the process noise with a normalised-innovation statistic.
- **Check.** On smooth MC engagements switching it off changes nothing (+0.006 s, p = 0.73).  On HB201-240 removing it costs +0.094 m pointing RMS [+0.062, +0.127].
- **Revision / outcome.** Kept because of the adversarial family.
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) no_adapt_q: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first -0.000 s [-0.001, +0.000] (p_Holm 1.000), d RMS +0.008 m [-0.001, +0.018] (p_Holm 0.696).

### D10 Calibration layer for P_hit  (outcome: rejected)

- **Specification.** The fire rule needs a calibrated hit probability.
- **Choice.** Isotonic map fitted on tuning seeds MC1-200.
- **Check.** Scored on MC5000-5199 (37 297 ticks, base rate 0.886): Brier 0.00353 raw -> 0.00353 calibrated; the Rao-Blackwellised posterior is already close to calibrated. *(Superseded 2026-11-15: the re-run in `outputs/calibration_study.txt` gives base rate 0.888 and Brier 0.00285 -> 0.00285; same conclusion.)*
- **Revision / outcome.** NOT ADOPTED, kept as a reported null: the identity table ships; the fitted candidate is outputs/calib_fit_candidate.m.txt.

### D14 Features with a measured null effect  (outcome: neutral)

- **Specification.** Value-aware bar after the first pulse (p_sure_after), n_upd_min = 4, posterior vs the 17-class hit model.
- **Choice.** Keep the principled versions; the 17-class model only as the comparison baseline.
- **Check.** p_sure_after and n_upd_min = 4 produced exactly zero difference on MC201-260; posterior vs 17-class differs by 9 ms.

### D12 Fire slip guard (sweep_guard)  (outcome: rejected)

- **Specification.** A safeguard for an evaluator that judges between calls: block shots when the beam-vs-target slip over one call period exceeds 0.5 m (my own idea).
- **Choice.** sweep_guard = 0.5 m.
- **Check.** Measured cost with no benefit: first-hit time 0.21 s at 100 Hz and 1.1 s at 20 Hz, pulse misses not increased when removed (MC201-260).
- **Revision / outcome.** REJECTED as default: sweep_guard = Inf; guard_window is kept so it is rate-independent if an evaluator ever needs it.
- **Final-design ablation** (`outputs/ablations.txt`) guard_on: 60/60 intercepted, 0 pulse miss(es), d t_first +0.207 s [+0.171, +0.243] (p_Holm 0.003), d RMS +0.000 m [+0.000, +0.000] (p_Holm 1.000).

### D15 The "correlated copies" failure: a harness bug, not an estimator weakness  (outcome: revised (harness fixed))

- **Specification.** Robustness to evaluator conventions for meas_valid (the "drift" convention: flag 1 on every call).
- **Choice.** First robustness run: 14 pulse misses and a median RMS of 2.56 m.  Raising min_gap (0.4 -> 0.9 radar periods) did not help.
- **Check.** That pointed away from the estimator: the harness kept meas_valid = 1 during blackouts, building the sample from a stale held measurement.
- **Revision / outcome.** REVISED: harness fixed; re-run MC201-230 gives 0 misses and median RMS 0.570 m at the default gap, so min_gap = 0.4 x 50 ms stays.

What that means for this problem
  Tracking a ramp-like LOS rate: the worst LOS rate is v/r = 150/800 = 0.1875 rad/s.  Feedback-only error with the type-1 loops: P (Kv 3.99) 0.0469 rad = 38 m at 800 m;
  P with K = 8012 (Kv 8.00) 0.0234 rad = 19 m at 800 m; the PI loop is type 2: zero ramp error.  (Budget: 2 m, i.e. 0.0025 rad at 800 m.)
  Noise: the controller gain seen by radar-rate noise (|C S| at the Nyquist 62.8 rad/s) is 1804 V/rad for the PI + roll-off, 4477 for P, 34425 for the Kv-8 lead (19.1x the PI; the Kv-2 lead: 7165).
  Margins: the P loop with the higher gain has GM 2.3 dB (Routh limit Kp < 10484); a lead raises the high-frequency gain by 1/alpha = 3.0 and moves the GM the wrong way: -5.7 dB at Kv 8 (unstable if negative), 5.0 dB even at Kv 2 (the P loop at Kv 2 has 14.4 dB).
  Why the chosen one wins: (1) the plant is type 1, so a ramp-like LOS rate leaves a finite feedback error 1/Kv with P or lead, and Kv is capped by the resonance gain limit; the PI adds the second integrator (type 2, zero ramp error);
  (2) the plant already has ~90 deg of phase at crossover, so there is no phase deficit for a lead to repair; (3) the roll-off pole keeps the radar-rate noise gain (and the resonance gain) low, which a lead does the opposite of;
  (4) the price of the PI is the 15 % overshoot of the loop step and a slow ~2.6 s tail (zero at -1/Ti), which the 2-DOF structure hides for the reference (exact inverse), see tc_study_feedforward_vs_feedback.

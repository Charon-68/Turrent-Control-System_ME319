LOS linearisation: theta = atan2(y,x), r^2 = x^2 + y^2
  d theta/dx = -y/r^2, d theta/dy = x/r^2  =>  d theta = (x dy - y dx)/r^2 = n.d / r,  n = [-sin th; cos th] (unit normal to the LOS)
  second-order term: -(x dx + y dy)(x dy - y dx)/r^4 + ..., so the relative error of the linear map is O(|d|/r)

Blackout (0.5 s) as a held reference: it delays the reference, not the loop
  worst LOS rate 0.1875 rad/s, worst LOS acceleration 0.0602 rad/s^2 (a/r + v^2/r^2 at 800 m)
  hold the last angle for 0.5 s: pointing error up to rate x 0.5 = 0.0938 rad = 75 m at 800 m (budget 2 m)
  hold the last angle AND rate (coast): error from the acceleration 0.5 a t^2 = 0.0075 rad = 6.0 m at 800 m (before the filter's own uncertainty, see outputs/error_budget.txt)
  -> a held angle misses the 2 m budget by 38x; extrapolating the estimated motion is what keeps the shot possible.  As a loop delay of 0.5 s the PM would be -49 deg (unstable): the loop does not contain it.

  The k-search is not monotone at the smallest delays (the 5 ms row allows a higher gain than Td = 0): a small delay moves the -180 deg crossing down in frequency, away from the resonance peak, which raises the gain margin
  in the first table too (16.1 -> 17.3 dB at 5 ms); from about 25 ms on the phase loss dominates.
  With no delay the limit is the resonance (55.9 rad/s, gain margin), not the delay: the safe crossover saturates near 12.3 rad/s at Td = 0.
  Rule of thumb that follows: wc x Td should stay under about 0.41 (rad) to keep 45 deg of margin from a starting PM of 68 deg.
